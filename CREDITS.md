# Credits

Every model and texture in `public/art` is from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under
[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain). The models were simplified and their textures
resized and re-encoded as WebP for the game; nothing else was changed. Fonts are self-hosted from `@fontsource` under the
SIL Open Font License. The tune is the traditional *Three Blind Mice* (public domain). Everything else (the mice, the
farmer's wife, the cat, the farmer's legs, the furniture built in code, the sound) is made in code in this repository.
